Core Gameplay

Jump. Dodge. Survive.

JumpNDodge is a fast-paced survival game where every second counts. Dodge obstacles, collect power-ups and beat your personal best.

Shop

Earn Coins. Unlock Rewards.

Use your hard-earned Coins to roll for powerful items and rare rewards. Every roll could contain something legendary.

Cosmetics

Make Your Run Yours.

Unlock unique trails and player cosmetics to customize your character and stand out from the crowd.

Statistics

Track Your Progress.

See your total playtime, rounds, jumps, deaths, Coins, 1-Ups and more. Find out how far you've really come.

Highscores

Beat Your Best.

Compete against your own records across different difficulties and push your score higher every run.

Difficulties

How far can you go?

Choose your challenge — from Easy all the way to Hell, with Dynamic difficulty changing the challenge as you play.

Powers

Change the Game.

Collect and equip different Powers to give yourself an advantage and completely change how you play.

Daily Rewards

Come Back Every Day.

Claim your daily reward and earn Coins with bonus rarity rewards. Keep coming back to build up your balance.

2-Player

Play Together. Fight Together.

Play locally with two players — work together in Co-Op or compete in VS, where only one player can survive.

Profiles

Your Game. Your Progress.

Create separate profiles with their own stats, achievements, highscores, settings, Powers and Cosmetics.

Custom Music

Play It Your Way.

Choose between different in-game music tracks and preview them before selecting your favorite.

Customization

Customize your experience with Dark Mode, fullscreen, sound settings, controls and multiple languages.

FAQ

Answers to the questions that get asked most.

Is JumpNDodge free?

Yes, completely free — no ads, no paywalls, no hidden costs.

Which platforms are supported?

Windows and Linux, via the installers above. There's currently no native macOS build.

Can I play with a friend?

Yes — locally, either in Co-Op or in the competitive VS mode.

Where do older versions or the portable build live?

All of that is in the Archive section below, downloadable directly without going through GitHub.

Will JumpNDodge get more updates?

Yes. The game is actively developed, with new features, cosmetics and challenges planned.
